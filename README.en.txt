PostNL brings your expected mail and parcels into Homey. View current deliveries with Homey devices, Flow cards and dedicated widgets.

Features

- My Post widget with recent mail scans and a local 21-day history.
- My Package widget with PostNL-orange package rows and a detailed package popup.
- Per-account devices with mail, parcel, delivery date and delivery window capabilities.
- Device-based Flow triggers, conditions, actions and tokens, including a trigger and reversible condition for a known delivery window.
- Automatic synchronization and a Timeline notification when an account must be reconnected.
