PostNL for Homey

Monitor expected mail and PostNL parcels in Homey.

Version 0.1.16 updates the My PostNL widget to show up to the 5 most recent mail items and 5 most recent parcels, including recently delivered parcels. The widget is scrollable and mail images can be opened larger.

Authentication is handled through the PostNL App Settings. Flow cards remain device-related and include rich tokens for mail and parcel events.
